# house_lord_placement:HL-7-in-8
11124	Connects marriage and partnership with transformation and shared resources.
11125	The lord of the 7th house — governing marriage, business partnership, public dealings, and the spouse — is placed in the 8th house, the house of transformation, occult matters, inheritance, and sudden events.
11126	Counted from the 7th house itself, the 8th house is the 2nd from it — a house of accumulation and continuation, suggesting that matters of marriage, business partnership, public dealings, and the spouse become bound up with sustaining or consolidating resources through transformation, occult matters, inheritance, and sudden events.
11127	In practice, this placement tends to channel the native's experience of marriage and partnership through the lens of transformation and shared resources, and the two areas of life should be read together rather than in isolation.
