10771	The 9th house is a trikona house — considered one of the two most auspicious houses in the entire chart, so this placement carries that house's general character in addition to the specific effects below.
10772	Counted from the 1st house itself, the 9th house falls in a trikona (trine) relationship to it.
10773	This is a classically auspicious connection, often linking self, physical body, personality, general vitality, and appearance with good fortune, grace, and support flowing in through fortune, dharma, the father, higher learning, and long journeys.
