# house_lord_placement:HL-5-in-8
11006	The lord of the 5th house — governing intelligence, creativity, romance, and speculation — is placed in the 8th house, the house of transformation, occult matters, inheritance, and sudden events.
11007	Counted from the 5th house itself, the 8th house falls in a kendra (angular) relationship to it.
11008	This is a stabilizing, mutually supportive functional connection, meaning matters of intelligence, creativity, romance, and speculation tend to find a firm, action-oriented outlet through transformation, occult matters, inheritance, and sudden events.
11009	In practice, this placement tends to channel the native's experience of creativity through the lens of transformation and shared resources, and the two areas of life should be read together rather than in isolation.
