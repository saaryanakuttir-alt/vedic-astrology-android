# house_lord_placement:HL-2-in-8
10828	Connects wealth and family with transformation and shared resources.
10829	The lord of the 2nd house — governing wealth, family, speech, food, and accumulated resources — is placed in the 8th house, the house of transformation, occult matters, inheritance, and sudden events.
10830	Counted from the 2nd house itself, the 8th house falls in a kendra (angular) relationship to it.
10831	This is a stabilizing, mutually supportive functional connection, meaning matters of wealth, family, speech, food, and accumulated resources tend to find a firm, action-oriented outlet through transformation, occult matters, inheritance, and sudden events.
10832	In practice, this placement tends to channel the native's experience of wealth and family through the lens of transformation and shared resources, and the two areas of life should be read together rather than in isolation.
