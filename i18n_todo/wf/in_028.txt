11664	Natural friend and temporally well-placed — the warmest possible relationship between two planets.
11665	Both the permanent (Naisargika) and the chart-specific (Tatkalika) friendship point the same way here, so a planet sitting in a Great Friend's sign is considered a welcome, supported guest: its significations tend to express with unusual ease, and it lends genuine strength to whatever it governs.
11666	Classically the strongest of the five Panchadha Maitri grades.

# panchadha_maitri:PM-Sama
11670	The natural and temporal readings pull in opposite directions and cancel out.

11672	A planet here is neither especially helped nor hindered by its host; read its other dignities (exaltation, own sign, etc.) as the more decisive factor.
