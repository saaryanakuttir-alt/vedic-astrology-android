# vimshottari_antardasha:AD-Ke-Ke
12731	During the Ketu Mahadasha, the Ketu Antardasha brings a sub-period of roughly 4.9 months in which the themes of detachment, spirituality, introspection, and intuition come into active focus, expressed within the broader, ongoing context of detachment, spirituality, introspection, and intuition that characterizes the overall Ketu Mahadasha.
# vimshottari_mahadasha:MD-Su
12732	A Sun mahadasha typically brings focus to self-identity, authority, status, career, government or official dealings, and the relationship with the father.
12733	It is a period where the native's confidence, leadership, and public standing are tested and, when supported, come to the fore.
12734	When the Sun is well-dignified (exalted, in its own sign, or otherwise well placed and unafflicted) and occupies a favorable house for the native's ascendant, this period tends to bring recognition, rise in authority or government favor, improved health and vitality, and leadership opportunities.
12735	When the Sun is debilitated, combust, or heavily afflicted, this period can instead bring ego conflicts, friction with authority figures or the father, setbacks to status or reputation, and health concerns connected to vitality, the heart, or bones.
# vimshottari_mahadasha:MD-Mo
12736	A Moon mahadasha emphasizes the mind, emotions, the mother, domestic life, public perception, and matters requiring adaptability.
12737	Because the Moon changes phase and sign quickly, this period's tone can vary noticeably depending on the Moon's strength at birth and by transit.
12738	When the Moon is strong (waxing, exalted, in its own sign, or otherwise well placed and unafflicted), this period tends to bring emotional stability, public popularity, domestic happiness, and success in fields involving the public, hospitality, or care-giving.
12739	When the Moon is weak (waning, debilitated, or afflicted), this period can bring emotional instability, anxiety, fluctuating health (particularly fluid-related), and difficulties connected to the mother or domestic peace.

# vimshottari_mahadasha:MD-Sa

12754	When Saturn is well-dignified (own sign, exalted, or otherwise well placed and unafflicted), this period rewards sustained, disciplined effort with steady, long-term achievement and authority earned through patience and responsibility.

# vimshottari_mahadasha:MD-Ra
12756	A Rahu mahadasha typically brings unconventional ambition, sudden changes, intensified material desire, and often a connection to foreign lands, technology, or unconventional paths to status.
12757	When Rahu is favorably placed (in a supportive house, not heavily afflicted, and well-disposed by its sign lord), this period can bring unconventional but substantial success, sudden rises in career or status, and gains connected to foreign or innovative ventures.

# vimshottari_mahadasha:MD-Ke
12759	A Ketu mahadasha typically brings detachment, introspection, and a turning away from material focus in whichever life area Ketu occupies, often accompanied by heightened intuition or a spiritual/psychic dimension to experience.
12760	When Ketu is favorably placed, this period can bring genuine spiritual growth, liberation from unhelpful attachments, and valuable, sometimes unexpected, intuitive insight or past-life talent surfacing.
12761	When Ketu is poorly placed or heavily afflicted, this period can bring isolation, confusion, sudden and hard-to-explain losses, and health concerns connected to vitality or the nervous system.
