# nakshatra:NK-SWA
11505	Vayu (god of wind)
11506	A young shoot bending in the wind, or coral
11507	Vayu's domain is movement without a fixed path, and Swati carries that: independence, adaptability, and a preference for self-direction over instruction.
11508	Flexible rather than rigid — like the young shoot in the symbol, it bends with pressure instead of breaking, and keeps moving on its own terms.
# nakshatra:NK-VIS
11509	Indra and Agni (jointly)
11510	A triumphal archway, or a potter's wheel
11511	The name means 'forked branch,' and the dual rulership (Indra's ambition, Agni's drive) fits a nakshatra defined by determined, sometimes single-minded pursuit of a goal — with a real choice-point along the way about which branch to commit to.
11512	Driven, competitive, and effective once the fork is resolved.
# nakshatra:NK-ANU
11513	Mitra (god of friendship and alliance)
11514	A lotus flower, or a triumphal archway
11515	Mitra rules alliance itself, and Anuradha reflects it: success that comes through cooperation, loyalty, and disciplined devotion to a shared goal or person rather than solo ambition.
11516	Warm and relationship-oriented, with real staying power once committed.
# nakshatra:NK-JYE
11517	Indra (king of the gods)
11518	A circular amulet, an umbrella, or an earring
11519	The name means 'the eldest/most senior,' and under Indra's kingly rulership this nakshatra carries real authority — along with the isolation and responsibility that often comes with being the one others lean on.
11520	Protective, capable of command, but prone to feeling the weight of that position.
# nakshatra:NK-MUL
11521	Nirriti (goddess of destruction and dissolution)
11522	A bunch of roots, or a lion's tail
11523	Mula literally means 'root,' and Nirriti's rulership makes this a nakshatra of getting to the bottom of things — sometimes by tearing down what's built on a false foundation first.
11524	Investigative, unafraid of destruction when it's in service of finding what's actually true underneath.
# nakshatra:NK-PAS
11525	Apas (goddess of the cosmic waters)
11526	An elephant's tusk, or a fan/winnowing basket
11527	The name means 'the early/invincible one,' and Apas's water rulership gives it a purifying, unstoppable-early-momentum quality — conviction that clears obstacles the way water wears down stone.
11528	Strong-willed, persuasive, and quick to gain ground once committed to a direction.
# nakshatra:NK-UAS
11529	The Vishvadevas (the universal/all-gods)
11530	An elephant's tusk, or the back legs of a small bed
11531	Where Purva Ashadha is early and forceful, Uttara Ashadha is the victory that lasts — earned slowly, through integrity, and recognized broadly (the universal-gods rulership) rather than won by force alone.
11532	Principled, patient, and durable in what it achieves.
# nakshatra:NK-SHR
11533	Vishnu
11534	An ear, or three footprints
11535	The name means 'hearing,' and the ear symbol is direct: this is a nakshatra of learning by listening — to teachers, to tradition, to what others have to say — and of connecting people through communication.
11536	Under Vishnu's preserving rulership, it favors continuity: passing knowledge on rather than inventing from scratch.
# nakshatra:NK-DHA
11537	The eight Vasus (gods of the elements and material wealth)
11538	A drum, or a flute
11539	The Vasus rule material abundance, and Dhanishta channels it through rhythm and shared celebration — music, group recognition, and a prosperity that's often public rather than private.
11540	Adaptable and socially attuned, with a real talent for timing.
# nakshatra:NK-SHT
11541	Varuna (god of the cosmic waters and cosmic law)
11542	An empty circle, or a hundred stars/flowers
11543	The name means 'a hundred physicians,' and Varuna's rulership over the unseen deep gives this nakshatra a secretive, investigative healing quality — comfortable working alone, drawn to what's hidden or not yet understood, and skeptical of anything too easily explained.
# nakshatra:NK-PBH
11544	Aja Ekapada (the one-footed goat/serpent, a fierce form of Rudra)
11545	A funeral cot's front legs, or a two-edged sword
11546	A fierce, intense nakshatra under a fierce, intense deity — spiritual or psychological transformation that comes through burning away what's false rather than gentle unlearning.
11547	Passionate, penetrating, sometimes uncomfortable to be near, but genuinely purifying in effect.
# nakshatra:NK-UBH
11548	Ahir Budhnya (the serpent of the deep)
11549	A funeral cot's back legs, or twin serpents
11550	Where Purva Bhadrapada burns, Uttara Bhadrapada settles — deep, restrained, and still, like the serpent coiled at the bottom of the cosmic ocean.
11551	Wisdom that comes from patience and depth rather than intensity; quiet endurance more than dramatic transformation.
# nakshatra:NK-REV
11552	Pushan (nourisher and protector of travelers and livestock)
11553	A fish, or a drum
11554	The zodiac's final nakshatra, and it plays the part: safe arrival, gentle completion, and a nourishing, protective quality especially toward those still in transit — literally Pushan's own domain.
11555	A soft landing after everything Ashwini through Uttara Bhadrapada has moved through.
