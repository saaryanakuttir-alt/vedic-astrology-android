# divisional_charts:DIV-D60
10713	past-life karma, fine-grained results
10714	Considered by many classical authorities (including Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra) to be the single most sensitive and important divisional chart for fine-grained confirmation of results and for understanding past-life karma (purva janma) carried into the current birth.
10715	Because of its extremely fine 0.5-degree resolution, it is highly sensitive to birth time accuracy.
10716	Given the 0.5-degree resolution, an accurate birth time (ideally verified/rectified) is essential; small birth-time errors of only a minute or two can shift the D60 sign, so this chart's reliability depends more heavily on precise input data than any other varga listed here.
