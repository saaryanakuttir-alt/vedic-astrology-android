# house_lord_placement:HL-6-in-8
11064	Connects service and obstacles with transformation and shared resources.
11065	The lord of the 6th house — governing obstacles, disease, debts, service, daily work, and competitors — is placed in the 8th house, the house of transformation, occult matters, inheritance, and sudden events.
11066	Counted from the 6th house itself, the 8th house falls in an upachaya (growth) relationship to it.
11067	Matters of obstacles, disease, debts, service, daily work, and competitors tend to develop and strengthen gradually through transformation, occult matters, inheritance, and sudden events, typically improving with sustained effort and time.
11068	In practice, this placement tends to channel the native's experience of service and obstacles through the lens of transformation and shared resources, and the two areas of life should be read together rather than in isolation.

11119	The lord of the 7th house sits in its own house, directly reinforcing marriage and partnership.
11120	The lord of the 7th house — governing marriage, business partnership, public dealings, and the spouse — is placed in the 7th house, the house of marriage, business partnership, public dealings, and the spouse.
11121	This is a Swakshetra-style placement: the lord of the 7th house sits within its own house.
11122	A house lord placed in its own house generally reinforces and stabilizes its own significations directly, strengthening marriage, business partnership, public dealings, and the spouse, provided the lord itself is reasonably well placed by sign and is not heavily afflicted by malefic influences.
11123	In practice, this placement tends to channel the native's experience of marriage and partnership through the lens of marriage and partnership, and the two areas of life should be read together rather than in isolation.
