# house_lord_placement:HL-3-in-8
10887	Connects courage and self-effort with transformation and shared resources.
10888	The lord of the 3rd house — governing courage, siblings, self-effort, short journeys, and communication — is placed in the 8th house, the house of transformation, occult matters, inheritance, and sudden events.
10889	Counted from the 3rd house itself, the 8th house falls in a dusthana (6th/8th/12th) relationship to it.
10890	This classically indicates that matters of courage, siblings, self-effort, short journeys, and communication may encounter obstacles, delay, loss, or significant transformation as they pass through transformation, occult matters, inheritance, and sudden events, unless other factors in the chart provide relief.
10891	In practice, this placement tends to channel the native's experience of courage and self-effort through the lens of transformation and shared resources, and the two areas of life should be read together rather than in isolation.
