10947	The lord of the 4th house — governing home, mother, property, vehicles, and emotional comfort — is placed in the 8th house, the house of transformation, occult matters, inheritance, and sudden events.
10948	Counted from the 4th house itself, the 8th house falls in a trikona (trine) relationship to it.
10949	This is a classically auspicious connection, often linking home, mother, property, vehicles, and emotional comfort with good fortune, grace, and support flowing in through transformation, occult matters, inheritance, and sudden events.
10950	In practice, this placement tends to channel the native's experience of home and inner comfort through the lens of transformation and shared resources, and the two areas of life should be read together rather than in isolation.
